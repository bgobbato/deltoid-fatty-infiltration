How to measure (step by step)
  1. Upload the shoulder CT: a .zip with the DICOM folder (or a .nii.gz). Patient name is removed on conversion.
  2. Series: the soft-tissue series opens first. Prefer it to bone kernels.
  3. Window: Muscle (W400/L50) to see the deltoid fascia.
  4. Reorient (right panel, step 5): click the centre of the humeral canal on a low axial slice (Shaft distal) and just below the surgical neck (Shaft proximal), then Reorient. A new series marked ⟂ opens with the axial plane perpendicular to the shaft.
  5. Reference slice: scroll the axial to the first slice at the superior greater tuberosity with a circumferentially corticated tuberosity, humeral head visible and no surgical neck. Tick the three criteria.
  6. Draw the whole deltoid (anterior + middle + posterior) along the inner fascia with Deltoid; close the outline, it fills on release. Paint hematoma, fluid or fragments with Exclude. Bone (cortex + marrow) is removed automatically.
  7. Calculate: CSA (mm²), mean HU, DFDI and fat %. Check the QC image. Download all results (CSV) for the statistics.

Method: Rescigno et al. 2026, Eur J Orthop Surg Traumatol 36:424 — one axial CT slice; DFDI = 0.5(−ZHU) + 0.5(−ZCSA). Higher = more degeneration. The DFDI shown uses the article's cohort mean/SD (approximation); recompute z-scores in your own cohort.
